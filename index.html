FAQ

Common questions

What is Lumo360?

Lumo360 is an AI interviewing platform that conducts natural, adaptive conversations with candidates and provides hiring teams with fully transparent, evidence-backed evaluation reports — so every decision is defensible.

How is Lumo360 different from other AI hiring tools?

Unlike black-box AI screening tools, Lumo360 makes every evaluation decision fully traceable. Your team can see the exact transcript moments that led to each recommendation — no unexplained scores.

Does Lumo360 replace human interviewers?

No. Lumo360 handles scalable first-round screening so your team can focus their time on the most promising candidates. Human judgement remains central — we just make it faster and more consistent.

How do I get started?

Book a demo directly on this page. The team will walk you through setup and help you configure your first screening workflow — typically live within a day.

Can AI really judge candidates fairly?

AI is not replacing human judgement. It standardises the first stage of screening so every candidate is evaluated against the same criteria. This reduces inconsistency and helps recruiters focus their time on the most relevant candidates.

Will candidates dislike using an AI interview system?

Most candidates don't experience it as a traditional "AI interview" or a one-way recording. Instead, it feels like a structured, conversational phone interview that they can complete on their own time. Many candidates actually prefer this format because it removes scheduling friction, provides clear expectations upfront, and allows them to complete the screening quickly without waiting days for a recruiter call.

Won't AI miss strong candidates?

Human screening already misses strong candidates due to fatigue, time pressure, and inconsistent evaluation. A structured, criteria-based AI screening reduces that variability and helps ensure candidates are assessed more consistently.

Why not just speak to all candidates directly?

You still speak to candidates who make it through the initial screening. The AI step removes repetitive early-stage calls that are focused on basic fit, so recruiters can spend more time on meaningful conversations with shortlisted candidates.

How do you handle fairness and compliance concerns?

All candidates are asked the same structured questions and assessed using a consistent, predefined rubric, which helps ensure fairness and reduce bias in early-stage screening. Lumo360 is designed with compliance in mind and is fully aligned with GDPR and the EU AI Act principles. Recruiters remain in full control of the process, with the ability to review all outputs before any candidate is progressed, ensuring transparency, accountability, and human oversight at every stage.

How much does Lumo360 cost?

Lumo360 is priced in credits: one credit is one CV assessment, ten credits is one full interview. Pay as you go is £0.50 per credit — so a full interview is £5.00 — for any amount from 1 to 10,000 credits. Prepaid bundles bring that down: Starter is 500 credits for £225 (£0.45 per credit, 10% off), Growth is 2,000 credits for £800 (£0.40, 20% off) and Scale is 6,000 credits for £2,100 (£0.35, 30% off). Enterprise volume agreements are quoted individually. New companies get 25 free credits to start. See pricing for the detail.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. Every new company gets 25 free credits: enough for 5 CV assessments and 2 full interviews. No card is required, the free credits don't expire, and it's one free trial per company, not per person. Credits you buy later are valid for 12 months from your latest purchase.

What happens if a candidate never completes their interview?

Credits are reserved when a candidate is invited and only settle once the interview completes. If someone drops out or never starts, those credits return to your balance. You pay for interviews that actually happen — not for invitations.
