What we collect and why

When you ask to start free or book a demo

Our form at lumo360.io/start asks for:

  • your name, job title and work email, and your phone number if you choose to give it;
  • your company's name, website and size;
  • your hiring: the type of organisation, roughly how many people you hire, where you hire, the kinds of roles, how you screen candidates today and anything else you choose to tell us;
  • whether you'd like a free trial or a demo, and whether you'd like product updates.

With it we record which page and button you came from, any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source) and the page that referred you.

We use this to reply to you, to decide whether to offer you free trial credits, to arrange a demo, and to understand which kinds of organisations are interested in Lumo360. We do this because you asked us to get in touch, and because it's in our legitimate interest to respond to business enquiries and understand our market.

Product updates

If you tick the box for product updates, we'll occasionally email you about Lumo360. This is based on your consent. Every email has a way to unsubscribe, and you can also ask us at any time.

When you browse the site

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't store anything on your device and doesn't identify you or follow you across other websites.

Like any website, our hosting provider processes your IP address to deliver pages to you and keep the site secure.

Cookies and similar technology

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so we don't ask you to accept any.

If you close the announcement bar at the top of the homepage, your browser remembers that choice on your own device so the bar stays closed. Nothing is sent to us.

Our fonts are served from our own website, so loading a page doesn't send your details to a font provider.

If we ever add cookies that need your agreement, we'll ask you first and update this notice.
